## Translations

Three deliberate translations. Each is recorded in the paper.

**Machines → stage formulas.** All the argument ever uses of "`T` has halted
within `y` steps" is that it is a `Δ₁` predicate of `y`, provably monotone.
The formalization therefore works with *stage formulas* (`StageFml`: a `Δ₁`
formula with a proof of provable monotonicity) rather than simulating Turing
machines. "`T` never halts" becomes "the stage formula is false at every
standard numeral". The paper notes this in Convention 2.1 and Remark 4.3.

**Machine dynamics → simultaneous fixed point.** The stage formulas depend on
the watched sentences only through their Gödel codes, which occur as closed
terms. So instead of a parametric self-referential formula, the development
takes the simultaneous fixed point (Foundation's `exclusiveMultifixedpoint`) of
the `2ⁿ` watched sentences; the stage formulas are then explicit and
non-self-referential. This is the paper's Remark 4.3.

**Models → consistency.** The paper says "there is a model of `PA + σ`"; the
Lean says `Consistent (T + σ)`. These are equivalent by the completeness
theorem, and the proof-theoretic form is what the paper's arguments establish.

One further difference, in the paper's favour: where the paper's Convention 2.1
assumes each proof has a uniquely determined conclusion, the formalization
instead has the trigger adopt the **least** pattern proved at a stage. The
tie-break makes the construction independent of any uniqueness convention. The
paper records this in the proof of Theorem 4.1.

## Axioms

Raw `#print axioms` output is archived in
[`RequestProject/TwoMinds/AXIOMS.txt`](RequestProject/TwoMinds/AXIOMS.txt) and
regenerated by CI on every push (see the `axiom-audit` artifact in the Actions
tab).

All fourteen named results have exactly the standard footprint:

```
[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]
```

**One caveat.** Instantiating the Main Theorem at `PA` itself — as opposed to
the generic `Δ₁`-axiomatized extension of `IΣ₁` that the development is stated
for — additionally depends on Foundation's `PA_delta1Definable`, which is a
literal `axiom` in that library (`Foundation/FirstOrder/Incompleteness/Examples.lean`)
stubbing the standard fact that `PA` is `Δ₁`-axiomatized. The generic
development is free of it. Foundation's `IΣ₁ ⪯ PA` and `Consistent PA` are
genuine instances, not stubs.

## Not formalized

* The final classification clause of Theorem 4.1 ("the only provable
  implications are those generated by `Con → Con_{Tᵢ}`"). Its remaining half
  needs `Consistent (PA + Con)`, i.e. `PA ⊬ ¬Con`, which follows from
  arithmetical soundness rather than from consistency alone. The paper scopes
  this explicitly in a footnote.
* Corollary 6.5 and Questions 6.6–6.9, which discuss open problems.
* Remark 5.1, which is expository.

No theorem statement was weakened to make a proof go through.

## Statements carry what they claim

`main` asserts all five properties the paper's Theorem 4.1 attributes to the
constructed family — never firing in ℕ, provable soundness, consistency of every
verdict pattern, necessitation, and extensional correctness over ℕ — so a reader
who destructures the existential recovers the full theorem rather than a
fragment. `two_minds` likewise asserts extensional correctness and provable
soundness of both predicates alongside the consistency claim; without those
clauses it would be satisfiable by a degenerate pair of stage formulas carrying
none of the paper's content, and the development includes a machine-checked
`example` recording that the cheap `topStage` witness is excluded.
